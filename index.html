Our Story

Three Generations of Goldsmiths

LP Jewellers began as a small family workshop, where every ring and necklace was shaped by hand over a charcoal flame. That same care shapes every piece we make today.

We work closely with each customer — from choosing the design to the final polish — so that what you wear carries a story, not just a shine.
